## Batch 1: from data we already hold

- [ ] **Employer identity across programs.** H-1B, wage-request and seasonal rows reach an
      employer page by text prefix today, so Intel's page counts 8,219 LCAs from Intellectt,
      Inteli Platforms and Inteliroute (39% over), and Apple's counts Apple Tree Dental and an
      Applebee's franchise group. Pure identity matching undercounts the other way
      (Salesforce.com, Inc. 5,645; "JPMorgan Chase &amp; Co"; FMR d/b/a Fidelity). The rule
      that gets both right on the top 40: identity after decoding HTML entities, keeping the
      legal name before "d/b/a", and dropping ".com". Prototype: prefix 676,333 vs 665,753, and
      every row it separates is a different legal entity. Build a nightly map
      (source slug -> page) and read every program line through it.
- [ ] **A page for every employer that files only H-1Bs or wage requests** (135,975 H-1B-only
      employers, 785,114 filings; 22,368 with 5+), folded with the seasonal-only pages into one
      non-PERM employer page that shows every program it files. Sitemap family, OG card,
      breadcrumbs, links, keeping every seasonal URL already published.
- [ ] **Law firms:** small identity rules (LLLP as a form word; a noise word glued to the
      last word, merged only when the result equals a bigger firm's key exactly), then firm
      pages across programs: H-1B LCAs and wage requests by firm, employers represented.
      Measured: Fragomen is one page of 48,322 with ~150 filings on 11 typo spellings.
- [ ] **Sponsor profile ranks** on employer pages, from a nightly per-employer index: each
      part ranked against other sponsors, warnings stated as facts. No single score.
- [ ] **Sponsor finder:** filter employers by recent PERM sponsorship, state, industry, wage
      level, share of transfer hires, cap-exempt status.
- [ ] **H-1B on city and occupation pages**, and city pages for cities that clear the floor
      on H-1B filings alone.
- [ ] **Transfer comparison:** two employers side by side plus the green card effect of
      switching, from primary sources (8 CFR 204.5(e), 205.1, INA 204(j), AC21 104(c)/106(a),
      8 CFR 214.2(h)(2)(i)(H)).
- [ ] **EAD extension page** with the Oct 30 2025 rule (FR 2025-19702) and the 2024 final rule
      it amended, read from the Federal Register.
- [ ] **Survey-wage search:** the LCA prevailing-wage source columns (verify the names with
      --dump-header first), a paced backfill, a case-search filter and a page.
- [ ] **Receipt-number list** kept in the visitor's browser on /uscis-case-status.
- [ ] **"Who's waiting"** view of community timelines by category and country.
- [ ] **Civil-surgeon link** where people meet the I-485 step.
