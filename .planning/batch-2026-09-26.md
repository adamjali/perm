# The Sep 26 2026 batch: one build, one deploy

Agreed with the owner on Sat Sep 26 2026 (8:00 to 8:10 AM EDT), extended by the competitor
sweep the same morning (the gaps it found are marked **S1** to **S14**). Built in the worktree
`~/cc/pt-build` on branch `build/sep26-everything`, never pushed until the end, because the

Rules for every lane:
- One deploy at the end. Full gates before (both typecheckers, full vitest, Python tests, a
  production build, e2e, rendered audits, phone widths, light and dark, Impeccable), and again
  against production after.
- **This machine is the 2015 laptop (8 GB, 4 cores).** Helper lanes run SCOPED vitest only (one
  file or one folder), `pnpm typecheck` at most once per finished item, no builds, no full suite,
  no browser of any kind. The coordinator runs builds, suites and every browser check, one at a
  time.
- No commits, no stashes, no `git checkout` in any lane. The coordinator commits.
- Registries are the coordinator's: `src/app/sitemap*.ts` and `src/lib/sitemap/*`,
  `src/lib/dataSections.ts`, `src/lib/pageCards.ts`, `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts`,
  `src/lib/datasetCoverage.ts`, `known-routes.json`, the glossary, the search palette's static
  index, `src/lib/constants/navigation.ts`, `vercel.json`, `src/app/api/cron/dispatch/jobs.ts`,
  `src/app/globals.css`. A lane returns the lines it needs as text in its handoff file.
- `convex/schema.ts`, `convex/http.ts`, `next.config.ts` and `convex/_generated/` belong to
  lane C (it is the only lane adding Convex tables and headers). Anyone else needing a change
  there asks lane C through the coordinator.
- Voice: contractions, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no filler; every claim sourced and
  dated; visual first, the important thing on top, detail behind a disclosure. Design stack on
  every UI piece (frontend-design, design-taste-frontend, Impeccable), house design system
  (`docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, lime `#2ECC40`, hard shadows), craft floor (16px body, labels
  14px or more with tracking up to .12em, 44px targets, reduced motion, keyboard, contrast).
- Public repo: no competitor names anywhere in code, copy, tests or `.planning/` (use "a rival",
  "rival A"). No owner name beyond what `/about` already says.
