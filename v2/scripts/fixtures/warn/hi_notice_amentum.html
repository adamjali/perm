WARN Notice – Amentum

Date Department Received WARN

August 20, 2026

Date of Closure or When Employees Will Be Affected

  • Layoff Effective: October 31, 2026 (unless extended by the government customer)

Number of Affected Employees (Total)

  • Total employees statewide: 62
  • Layoff is not temporary
  • No bumping rights exist

Company/Employer Information

  • Company Name: Amentum
  • Worksite Address: Fernandez Hall, Building 580, Schofield Barracks, HI 96857
  • Contact Person: (removed) Additional Information: The government customer eliminated the existing contract and awarded it to another company.

Summary of the Notice

Amentum has notified the State of Hawaii, under the Hawaii Dislocated Workers Act, that the government customer for the Empty Pockets contract has elected to eliminate the contract and award the work to another company. This decision results in the layoff of 62 Amentum employees located at Fernandez Hall, Building 580, Schofield Barracks, Hawaii.

The layoffs are scheduled to take place on October 31, 2026, unless the government customer extends the contract. Amentum states this is not a temporary layoff and no bumping rights exist for any employee.
