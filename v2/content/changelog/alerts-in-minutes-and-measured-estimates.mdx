This week made the alerts faster and put a measurement behind every date the
site gives.

## Alerts within minutes

A watched case is checked every 5 minutes on weekdays from 7 AM to 9 PM
Eastern, and every half hour otherwise. A status change is emailed at once.
DOL emails the filer the moment it decides, so the attorney still hears first;
this gets as close as anyone can after that.

A case alert keeps watching for a year after DOL decides, so an appeal or a
reversal is emailed too.

## Every date, measured

- **The PERM range** is measured every night against how far recent decisions
  landed from their dates, by how far out the case is, and it's never wider than
  60 days.
- **A case DOL's queue has passed** is dated by the rate DOL actually finishes
  those cases.
- **A wage request** gets a day, from the requests ahead of it and DOL's
  measured pace.
- **A case at RFI** is dated from the day its RFI was issued.
- **H-2B applications** in DOL's assignment groups are dated by their group.

[The scorecard](/estimate-scorecard) shows how each one is doing.

## The busiest H-1B employers

[H-1B employers](/h1b-employers) ranks the 100 busiest employers for each fiscal
year and state, two ways: by DOL's certified LCAs, where the job is, and by
USCIS's approvals, where the employer is.

## For developers

The [API](/developers) gained keys with scopes and expiry, sandbox keys,
exports, live DOL lookups and signed webhooks. Everything is free for now.

## Fixes

- A page left open in a tab or on a phone's home screen now refreshes its
  figures when you come back to it after half an hour.
- Articles and the FAQ quote DOL's current average and queue month with the date
  DOL published them, where they had carried August's figures.
- Comparison tables and step numbers were missing from 47 articles; they show
  again.
- [Filing months](/perm-queue) lead with the share of the month DOL has decided,
  and the case page draws the line ahead of a case month by month.
