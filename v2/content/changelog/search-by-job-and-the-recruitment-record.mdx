This week added a search by job, DOL's record of how each case recruited, and the deadlines after a denial.

## Find a sponsor by job

[Find a sponsor](/sponsor-finder) now searches by job as well as by name: pick an occupation and a state to see the employers that file PERM for it, with their approval rate and typical wait where they have enough cases.

## Where employers recruit, and when

DOL's newer PERM form records each case's recruitment: the newspapers, the job order and the extra steps. [PERM recruitment](/perm-recruitment) shows it for the whole country, and each employer, law firm and job page shows its own.

## A denied ETA 9089

A case with a denial now has its own dates: 30 days from the letter to ask for reconsideration or BALCA review, on the dashboard, the case card, the calendar and in reminders.

## Drawings that move

Guides on the filing window, DOL's queue, a wage determination's validity, an RFI and the visa bulletin carry a drawing that runs on the real figures, with a pause button. Each refreshes when its data does.

## Faster pages

Employer, law firm and job pages open several times faster than a week before, and pages load only what you're about to open.

## Fixes

- The wage determination's expiry limits a case only while recruitment hasn't begun inside it (20 C.F.R. 656.40(c)). Cases that began recruiting in time no longer show the filing window cut short.
- The calendar calls the certification's expiry the I-140 deadline, as the dashboard does.
- Every page says when it last changed, the same day everywhere.
- The deadline calculator's dates can be copied, added to a calendar or saved as a case.
