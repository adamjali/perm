PERM Tracker now serves both halves of a PERM case: the wait and the work.

## The data surface

The site opens with two doors. If you are waiting on a case, the
[data overview](/tools) shows where DOL's queue stands today, from the
Department's own published figures with the date they carry. If you manage
cases, the tracker computes every deadline the regulations fix.

What's live:

- **[The overview](/tools)** — DOL's current position, the average to a
  determination, and the prevailing wage backlog, drawn as a tape.
- **[Six calculators](/calculators)** — decision estimates, the wage queue,
  the I-140 backlog, priority dates against the visa bulletin, the whole
  green card to scale, and every statutory deadline in a case.
- **[Processing times](/perm-processing-times)** — every queue DOL publishes,
  and a movement record that grows as we store each new snapshot.
- **[Methodology](/methodology)** — why public estimators disagree by months
  on the same case, where our figures come from, and what we refuse to
  publish.

## One email when your month comes up

Set a [queue alert](/perm-processing-times) with your filing month. When
DOL's analyst review queue reaches it, we send one email. It is not a
newsletter, and nothing else follows it.

## Faster

The homepage dropped its largest image payload, the demo player now loads
only when you scroll to it, and stylesheets are cached across pages instead
of shipped inside every one. First loads and navigation are both quicker.
