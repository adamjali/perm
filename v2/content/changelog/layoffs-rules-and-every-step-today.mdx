This week widened the record around a case: layoffs in nearly every state, the rules on their way, and one page for where every step stands.

## Layoff notices from 47 states and DC

[Layoffs](/layoffs) reads the WARN notices every state publishes that a script may read, 47 states and DC, and links each notice to its employer's page. The three states that publish no list are named, with the reason from their own pages.

## Rules on their way

[Policy changes](/policy-changes) follows each immigration rule through White House review, public inspection and the Federal Register, with its comment deadline and effective date. It also reads the White House's own actions, USCIS's and State's news, bills in Congress, and court and BALCA decisions, each dated and linked.

## The answer first

The [homepage](/) opens with the filing month DOL is deciding, then a chart of every filing month, decided and still waiting, then the case box.

## Where every step stands today

[Today](/today) puts the wage request, PERM, I-140 and visa bulletin on one line: where each queue is, whether it's moving faster or slower, and what changed. [PERM by the numbers](/perm-by-the-numbers) adds the totals DOL's own case records add up to.

## Trackers compared

[Compare](/compare) sets PERM Tracker beside the other trackers people search for, each mark quoted from the tracker's own page.

## Reviews

[Reviews](/reviews) are left on the site's own form. Every one is shown except spam, abuse, and reviews from people who work on the site.

## Fixes

- One colour code everywhere: certified green, denied red, waiting yellow, withdrawn purple.
- The contact form filters spam without ever losing a real message.
- Sign-up is simpler, and the alert forms say no account is needed.
