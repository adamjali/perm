This release adds the steps around the PERM: a page for each USCIS form, the consulate's wait, and the law firms and employers behind the filings.

## A page for each USCIS form

Each of 15 forms has its own page, from the [I-140](/uscis-processing-times/i-140) to the [N-400](/uscis-processing-times/n-400): USCIS's median months quarter by quarter, what it took in, decided and had waiting, and its yearly medians since fiscal 2016. The [processing times page](/uscis-processing-times) links each one.

## How long the consulate takes

[Visa interview waits](/visa-wait-times) shows the State Department's own table of how long each embassy and consulate takes to interview, with India and China first. Every reading is kept, and a post that prints no figure says so rather than showing zero.

## Who files, and who's exempt

- [Law firm rankings](/law-firm-rankings) lists the busiest firms each fiscal year, for PERM and for H-1B, nationally and in every state, each linked to its page. Firms only, never a person.
- [Cap-exempt employers](/h1b-cap-exempt-employers) comes from the employer's own answer on DOL's wage request form, which asks whether a university or research exemption covers it. USCIS decides, so each is worded as likely.

## Your case, in more detail

- A case page lists every status DOL has shown for it, the day each changed and how long each lasted.
- [Email alerts](/perm-case-alerts) puts every free alert on one page: your case, your filing month, the visa bulletin and any employer.
- "How long does PERM take right now?" is answered from today's readings, on the [case lookup](/perm-case-status) and [processing times](/perm-processing-times) alike.

## Wages, further back

The [wage level tool](/tools/wage-levels) now shows where one yearly wage reaches each level in every wage area of a state. A job's history reaches back to July 2016, under the job codes DOL used until June 2022 wherever the code changed.

## DOL's quarterly file, read

Each quarterly PERM file gets a note of its own. The first reads [April to June 2026](/blog/dol-perm-file-fy2026-q3) case by case, against the quarter before and a year earlier.

## Fixes

- Pictures shared from the site no longer carry figures that go out of date. A card over a live page shows the page's shape, its numbers drawn as grey bars.
- [Compare trackers](/compare) covers four more, each read on its own pages.
- Three pages said something wrong, now in the [corrections log](/changelog/corrections): the privacy policy's line about the chat's AI providers, the homepage's PERM wait, and "teams" on the attorney page.
