The quarterly disclosure files carry far more than timing, and the data
surface now shows it.

## By state

[An interactive map](/perm-by-state) of every filing in the current window,
placed at its worksite state - real Census geometry, not a diagram. Hover
reads a state, a tap pins it: filings, certifications, approval rate,
median days and median wage. The ranked table below it carries the exact
figures.

## Wages

[Median offered wages by occupation](/perm-wages). These are wages employers
committed to in federal filings, which makes them harder currency than any
salary survey. Hourly and other wage units are annualized before the median
is taken, and every occupation shows its volume, approval rate and median
days beside the wage.

## Employers

[The hundred biggest sponsors](/perm-employers), ranked and searchable, each
with its filings, certifications, approval rate and median days. Names
appear exactly as DOL prints them.

## The same discipline as everything else

All three pages come from the same unioned, de-duplicated disclosure files
as the processing-time medians - 250,000+ real cases - and the
[methodology page](/methodology) shows each figure's recipe. Nothing is
modeled, nothing is invented, and where the data can’t answer, the page
says so.
