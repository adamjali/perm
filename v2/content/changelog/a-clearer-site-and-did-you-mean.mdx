This week made the site easier to read and easier to search, and added USCIS's own numbers on requests for evidence.

## A clearer look

The site has one bright green for the main action on each page, and fewer boxes, shadows and moving parts around it. Every page's figures sit in one place, with the details folded underneath. The signed-in app got the same treatment, and every picture of it was taken again.

## Every picture is its own

Each guide and blog post now has a picture of the thing it explains: the government form, the page on this site, or the app itself, credited and dated. No two articles share one.

## "Did you mean"

A search that comes up empty now offers the closest names: a typo, swapped letters or a missing space still finds the employer, law firm or job. "amazn" finds Amazon. The same works in the site search, the case search, every list and the page-not-found screen.

## Articles by topic

[Guides](/guides) and the [blog](/blog) are filed by topic, so the guides on case status, the queue, deadlines or wages sit together.

## USCIS's RFE rates

[RFE rates](/uscis-rfe-rates) shows how often USCIS asked for more evidence on each I-129 work visa, by month since October 2014, from USCIS's own quarterly files.

## Fixes

- [RFI issued](/guides/perm-rfi-issued-what-to-do) says what happens next, step by step, from the cases our nightly check watched DOL move.
- A bar on a chart opens the cases behind it.
- A page translated by the browser keeps working.
- A case number in an employer's filings list is big enough to tap on a phone.
