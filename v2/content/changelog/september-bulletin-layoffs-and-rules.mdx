The first half of September added the context around a case: what the
bulletin is likely to do, who is laying people off, what a job has to pay,
and which rules are about to change.

## The visa bulletin, computed from its own history

[The visa bulletin page](/visa-bulletin) shows what every earlier bulletin for
the coming month did, category by category, beside USCIS's count of the people
waiting ahead of each cutoff. It doesn't forecast. The day a bulletin lands,
the page leads with what that bulletin did. The family-sponsored charts have
their own page ([family bulletin](/visa-bulletin/family)), and the year's
spillover from unused family numbers is read from the State Department's own
annual limits sheet and Table V.

## Layoff notices

[Layoffs](/layoffs) lists the WARN notices California, New York, Texas and
Washington publish, linked to the employer's page wherever the company also
sponsors PERM cases. Texas refuses scripts, so its file is loaded by hand;
the page says so.

## What a job has to pay

[Wage levels](/tools/wage-levels) asks DOL's own wage search for the four
levels of any occupation in any area, the OEWS figures DOL draws on for a
prevailing wage determination.

## Rules, with their dates

[Policy changes](/policy-changes) lists the Federal Register documents and OFLC
announcements that touch these programs, each with its effective date, its
comment window, the printed first page and the document's own DATES paragraph. A
timeline shows what is open for comment and what takes effect next.

## Smaller additions

- [The glossary](/glossary) and [the status dictionary](/perm-case-statuses)
  define every term and every status DOL uses, with the rule each one comes
  from.
- [Debarments](/debarments) now separates a bar in force, a bar that ended and
  a bar ordered for a future date. One employer had been shown as "ended" 52
  days before its bar began.
- [Badges](/badges) put DOL's published figures in a README or a web page.
- Every email links to its own way out: subscriber mail to the [preference
  page](/email-preferences), account mail to Settings.
- [Corrections](/changelog/corrections) lists everything this site published
  that was wrong, what was true, and what changed.
