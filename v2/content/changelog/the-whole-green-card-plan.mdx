This release plans the whole road to a green card from your own case, and lets every count on the site open the cases behind it.

## The whole plan, from your own dates

The [green card timeline](/tools/green-card-timeline) takes whatever dates your case has, from the wage request on, and dates every step after them as a range from today's DOL and USCIS figures. Each length and assumption can be changed. For a green card through a consulate abroad, it dates the National Visa Center's steps from State's own weekly timeframes. It works out the visa bulletin wait two ways, from how fast the cutoff moves and from how many people are ahead, and lists every government fee on the day it falls due, and which of them the law puts on the employer.

## What moved since yesterday

A [filing month](/perm-queue) shows how many of its cases moved since the end of the day before, by status. Every status and every employer initial opens the month's cases, and [the queue](/perm-queue) can go back to any day since August 30. A line chart shows the analyst review queue day by day, and [PERM by the numbers](/perm-by-the-numbers) shows cases filed and decided each month since 2024.

## Each PERM tied to its wage request

A decided PERM now names the wage request behind it, and its page shows the dates in between: the determination, the job order, each ad, the quiet period and the filing. Employer, law firm and job pages say how long they take from a wage request to the PERM, and an employer page shows its headcount and founding year from its own filings.

## Every step's pace

[Today](/today) shows each step's wait in days, how fast its queue is moving, and how its wait changed in the last 30 days.

## EB-2 or EB-3, by the numbers

How many PERM jobs meet the EB-2 bar, which line employers actually filed in by country, and where the two lines stand: [a data note](/blog/eb2-vs-eb3-by-the-numbers), read live.

## How we check what we publish

[Editorial standards](/editorial-standards) says where every figure comes from, who writes the guides and how mistakes are corrected. Every article and every data page links it, the methodology and a way to report an error, and every section of every guide now opens with its answer.
