Late September reached past DOL to the steps that follow a PERM, and made the
alerts quieter and more useful.

## USCIS, from its own quarterly files

- [USCIS processing times](/uscis-processing-times): every form's median, quarter
  by quarter, from USCIS's performance data.
- [I-485 by field office](/i485-by-field-office): what each office received,
  approved, denied and holds, and how many quarters of work it holds at its pace.
- [Approved I-140s awaiting a visa](/i140-awaiting-visa): the count by category
  and country of birth.

Each file is checked against its own totals before anything is stored.

## Follow an employer

Every employer page has a follow box. It emails when DOL moves that employer's
cases as a group: a batch put on hold or released, or a day of decisions well
above the employer's own pace. [Employers under review](/perm-employers/under-review)
shows the same moves, dated, and names who acted: a hold is DOL's, an appeal is
the employer's.

## One alert email a day

Several updates for one address now wait and go out together, at most one
email a day.

## Estimates that are graded

[The estimate scorecard](/estimate-scorecard) records a sample of our estimates
every day, before the outcome, and grades each one once DOL decides. Nobody
picks the cases.
