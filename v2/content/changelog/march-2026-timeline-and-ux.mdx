## What's New

### Enhanced Timeline View

The Gantt chart now visualizes your entire PERM timeline, covering every date range and milestone that matters:

- **Range bars** for PWD Validity, NOF Period, Filing Window, and ETA 9089 Certification
- **Milestone markers** for Audit Selected, I-140 Receipt, and I-140 Denied
- **Stacked rows** so overlapping ranges are readable at a glance
- **Dashed borders** distinguish calculated dates from dates you entered

Each range bar is color-coded and labeled inline, so you can scan across cases without hovering or clicking.

### Smarter AI Chat

The AI assistant now uses a 5-model fallback chain that automatically switches providers when one is unavailable. Conversations are more reliable, context stays intact across longer threads, and you'll see fewer interruptions during peak usage.

## Improved

### Redesigned Homepage

The landing page has been rewritten with clearer messaging about what PERM Tracker actually does and why deadlines matter. Less marketing fluff, more substance.

### Cleaner Interface

A proportional scale-down across the app reduces visual clutter without removing any functionality. Cards, spacing, and typography are tighter and more balanced, especially on smaller screens.

### Better Deadline Hints

Recruitment deadline tooltips now explain *why* a date matters. Each hint includes the regulatory basis so you can verify the logic yourself.

### Range Bar Labels

Timeline range bars now always show their labels instead of only on hover. No more guessing which bar represents which date range.

## Fixed

- **Filter bar** now appears when all cases are archived, previously showed the new-user empty state by mistake
- **"Clear All Filters"** correctly persists the cleared state across page navigation
- **Unknown routes** now show a proper 404 page instead of redirecting to login
- **Deadline enforcement** runs daily to automatically close expired deadlines, no more stale open cases
