Many firms pair a forms tool with something that keeps the PERM dates, because few forms suites name the PERM clocks on their own pages.

## Immigration case management suites

**INSZoom** is the enterprise tool for corporate immigration departments and multi-office firms, owned by Mitratech since November 2020. It e-files USCIS and DOL forms. Pricing comes from a demo.

**LawLogix**, owned by Equifax since August 2022, pairs case management with I-9 and E-Verify compliance. Pricing comes from a demo.

**Docketwise** is built around smart forms, questionnaire intake and a client portal, with USCIS updates by receipt number and priority date tracking. It's quick to start with, and its pages don't name the PERM dates.

**eImmigration** and **LollyLaw** are forms-led suites in the same space. eImmigration lists DOL filing among its features and imports from other platforms, Docketwise included.

**CampLegal** is the one suite we read whose own employment immigration page names PERM recruitment, the audit file and prevailing wage management.

## PERM-specific tracking

For PERM dates on their own, PERM Tracker is a free tool built only for PERM labor certification.

### PERM Tracker

A free tool built only for PERM labor certification. Enter a case's dates once, and it works out the recruitment deadlines, the filing window under [20 C.F.R. § 656.17(e) (2025)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/chapter-V/part-656/subpart-C/section-656.17), the wage determination's expiration and the I-140 deadline after certification, with reminders by email and push and a sync to Google Calendar. It imports a caseload from JSON and exports to CSV or JSON.

**Suits**: any practice filing PERM, from a solo practitioner to a high-volume program
**Doesn't do**: forms, billing, or USCIS filing; it sits beside the tools that do
**Pricing**: Free

## Practice management with immigration add-ons

**Clio** is general legal practice management: billing, timekeeping, documents and a large set of integrations. Immigration work runs on it through customization rather than built-in workflows.

**MyCase** covers billing, documents and a client portal, and its Immigration Add-On adds forms, USCIS tracking and e-filing.

## How to choose

The common setup depends on the practice's size and how much PERM it files. For a solo practice that's mostly PERM, it's PERM Tracker with Clio or MyCase for billing.

| Practice | A common setup |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Solo, mostly PERM | PERM Tracker (free) with Clio or MyCase for billing |
| Solo, general immigration | Docketwise, eImmigration or Clio |
| Small firm (2 to 10 attorneys) | A forms suite, with PERM Tracker for PERM cases |
| Mid-size firm | LawLogix, INSZoom or CampLegal |
| Corporate immigration department | INSZoom or LawLogix |
| High-volume PERM, 100+ filings a year | PERM Tracker for the dates beside a forms suite, or CampLegal |

## What to ask any vendor

Ask whether it works out the PERM dates or only stores them, whether it reminds you, and what it costs per user.

1. Does it work out the PERM filing window from the first and last recruitment dates, or only store the dates you type?
2. Does it hold the prevailing wage determination's expiration date, and the I-140 deadline after certification?
3. Does it remind you before a date passes, by email, in the app, or on your calendar?
4. Does it keep the audit file together, and can you export every case, with its dates, if you leave?
5. What does it cost per user, billed yearly and monthly, and what does moving your data in cost?

A demo built on one of your own recent cases, with its real dates, answers most of these in an hour.

## The short version

Use the tool that's best at each part of the work: forms, deadlines, billing and compliance. What matters most is a system that tells you before a date passes, whichever software it runs on.
