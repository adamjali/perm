This is for solo attorneys, small firms and nonprofits looking for immigration software that costs nothing. The honest answer, from each vendor's own website on October 16, 2026: none of the full forms-and-case suites we read is free. They offer trials of 7 to 14 days, two publish nonprofit discounts, and the free tools that do exist each cover one part of the work.

<Callout type="stat" stat="7 to 14 days" title="The free trials we found">
Docketwise and LollyLaw offer 7 days, MyCase 10, and CampLegal, eImmigration and CaseTracker 14. INSZoom and LawLogix offer a demo on request.
</Callout>

A trial is for testing, so test the hard part. For a PERM practice that means entering one real case with its recruitment dates and checking what the product does with them.

## Nonprofit pricing

Two vendors publish a nonprofit price on their own pages:

| Vendor | What its own page says |
|---|---|
| eImmigration | "Non-profit organizations receive 50% off the standard monthly rate when committing to an annual term. Schools and universities also qualify for this discount." |
| Docketwise | "Non-profit organizations benefit from an additional 18.5% discount on our annual plan," for 501(c)(3) organizations (help center article dated September 15, 2026) |

We found no nonprofit price on the pricing, trial or FAQ pages of LollyLaw, CampLegal or MyCase, or on Mitratech's current INSZoom page. If you're a legal aid organization, ask each vendor directly; an unpublished discount can still exist.

## What's free, permanently

Four things stay free: the agencies' own status lookups, PERM Tracker's case lookup and calculators, PERM Tracker's app, and a spreadsheet.

<StepByStep>
<Step number={1} title="The agencies' own status lookups">
DOL's [case status search](https://flag.dol.gov/case-status-search) answers a DOL case number, and USCIS's [Case Status Online](https://www.uscis.gov/tools/checking-your-case-status-online) takes the 13-character receipt number. Both are free and need no account.
</Step>
<Step number={2} title="PERM Tracker's case lookup and calculators">
The [case lookup](/perm-case-status) reads DOL's status for any PERM, wage request, LCA, H-2A, H-2B or CW-1 number, with an estimated decision date and an email when DOL moves the case. The [calculators](/tools) work out PERM deadlines, prevailing wage validity and RFI due dates. No account needed.
</Step>
<Step number={3} title="PERM Tracker's app, for a PERM caseload">
Free, and PERM-only. Enter each case's dates once and it works out the recruitment deadlines, the filing window and the I-140 deadline, with reminders, Google Calendar sync and CSV or JSON export. Details on [For attorneys](/for-attorneys).
</Step>
<Step number={4} title="A spreadsheet">
Free, and fine for a handful of cases, but every date and every deadline is typed and checked by hand. [Manual vs automated tracking](/guides/manual-vs-automated-tracking) sets out where that breaks.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## What PERM Tracker's free app doesn't do

It's built for one thing. It doesn't fill USCIS or DOL forms, bill clients or keep a trust account, file with USCIS, or run a client portal. A firm that does family, humanitarian or nonimmigrant work beyond PERM will still want a forms suite, and the app can sit beside one. For what the paid suites cost, see the [2026 pricing table](/blog/immigration-software-pricing-2026).

## The costs that come with "free"

The costs come later: getting your data out, moving cases in, and the security questions for anyone holding clients' files.

- **Your data at the end.** Before a trial ends, check what an export contains. If it's partial, re-entering the cases is the real cost.
- **Migration.** Moving cases in later isn't always free: eImmigration lists $1,000 for a standard import, and Docketwise's pricing page lists free migration on its annual Advanced plan.
- **Security.** Free or paid, the same questions apply to anyone holding clients' files. See [what to ask a vendor about your clients' data](/blog/vendor-data-security-questions).

Prices and trial terms change. Every figure here is dated October 16, 2026, and the vendor's own page is the authority if it differs today.
