One figure isn't comparable with the rest. CaseTracker charges an account a monthly price by how many cases it watches, with unlimited team members, so a five-person firm pays the same $100 as a solo practitioner with the same caseload.

## What a per-user price leaves out

<StepByStep>
<Step number={1} title="Add-ons">
MyCase's Immigration Add-On is a separate license. eImmigration's Starter and Essentials plans can add premium features at $15 per feature, per user a month. Read what the plan you'd actually use includes.
</Step>
<Step number={2} title="Getting your data in">
eImmigration lists $1,000 for a standard import. Docketwise lists free migration on its annual Advanced plan, and CampLegal says implementation and migration are included. The rest quote it.
</Step>
<Step number={3} title="Yearly versus monthly">
Every vendor that publishes both charges less per month on a yearly term, from about 8% less (eImmigration Starter) to 20% less (LegistAI).
</Step>
<Step number={4} title="Seats you'd forget">
If paralegals and assistants log in, they're users too. At $80 a seat, a firm of two attorneys and three staff pays for five.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

## What none of these prices buy for PERM

We read each vendor's pricing and feature pages for the PERM deadlines: the filing window, at least 30 days after the last mandatory recruitment step and within 180 days of the first (20 C.F.R. § 656.17(e) (2025)); the notice of filing; the audit file kept 5 years (§ 656.10(f)); and the I-140 due within 180 days of certification (§ 656.30(b)(1)). Only CampLegal's employment immigration page names PERM work directly ("Recruitment tracking, audit file maintenance, and prevailing wage management built into the workflow"). The others name forms, USCIS tracking and portals.

PERM Tracker's app is free and PERM-only. It works out each case's recruitment and filing dates and the I-140 deadline, with reminders and calendar sync. It isn't a forms tool, a billing tool or a USCIS portal, so a firm that uses it keeps a forms suite for the forms. See [For attorneys](/for-attorneys).
