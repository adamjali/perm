## What each says about PERM

None of the four pages we read names the PERM deadlines themselves: the filing window, at least 30 days after the last mandatory recruitment step and within 180 days of the first (20 CFR 656.17(e)); the notice of filing's 10 business days (656.10(d)); the audit file kept 5 years (656.10(f)); or the I-140 due within 180 days of certification (656.30(b)(1)). Here's the closest each comes.

| Vendor | Closest it comes to PERM, in its own words |
|---|---|
| Docketwise | "Case & Priority Date Tracking", and DOL forms in its library |
| INSZoom | E-filing of "USCIS and DOL forms" |
| LollyLaw | "40+ immigration-specific workflows that come out-of-the-box with tasks, deadlines, milestones" |
| eImmigration | Filing to "USCIS, DOS, and DOL websites without retyping any information" |

A product can still hold a PERM case: workflows and custom tasks can carry dates. The deadline logic just isn't something these vendors advertise, so ask for it in a demo, on one of your own cases.

## How to choose

<StepByStep>
<Step number={1} title="Start with what you file most">
A practice that's mostly family and humanitarian work needs forms, translations and a client portal first. A PERM-heavy practice needs the recruitment and filing dates kept right.
</Step>
<Step number={2} title="Demo on a real case">
Bring one recent PERM with its real dates and ask the vendor to show the filing window, the notice of filing and the I-140 deadline in their product.
</Step>
<Step number={3} title="Ask about leaving before you arrive">
Ask what an export contains, in what format, and what a migration costs. eImmigration publishes its import price; ask the others.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

If the forms suite you prefer doesn't keep the PERM dates, PERM Tracker's free app can sit beside it: it works out each case's recruitment and filing dates and reminds you before they pass.
