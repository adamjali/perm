This is for attorneys and paralegals who answer "any update on my PERM?" several times a day. Most of those emails ask for one thing DOL already publishes, the case's status, and the client can read it without you: by case number, for free, with an email when DOL moves the case.

<Callout type="tip" title="What the client needs">
Just the DOL case number. Current ones look like G-100-26125-868956; cases filed in 2022 and 2023 use a shorter form like A-23043-00641. It's on the ETA-9089 filing receipt. Prevailing wage, LCA, H-2A, H-2B and CW-1 numbers work in the same box.
</Callout>

## Three ways to set it up

Send the client the lookup link once, let them turn on the email alert, or put the case checker on your own site.

<StepByStep>
<Step number={1} title="Send the link once">
At engagement, or when the ETA-9089 is filed, send the client the case number and permtracker.app/perm-case-status. The lookup needs no account.
</Step>
<Step number={2} title="Let them turn on the alert">
On the case page, "Email me changes" sends them an email when DOL moves the case. They confirm by email first, and one click turns it off. A status change is emailed at once.
</Step>
<Step number={3} title="Or put the checker on your own site">
The case checker runs inside any web page. It's free, and the code is on the [badges and embeds page](/badges#embeds) and in [For attorneys](/for-attorneys#for-your-site).
</Step>
</StepByStep>

The checker is one iframe and a source line:

## A reply to keep on file

For the emails that still come in, a pre-written answer saves the context switch. Adjust it to your firm's voice:

> Thanks for checking in. Your PERM is with the Department of Labor under case number G-100-XXXXX-XXXXXX. You can see its current status any time at permtracker.app/perm-case-status, and turn on an email for when DOL moves it. DOL's own search is at flag.dol.gov/case-status-search. We'll be in touch as soon as there's anything you need to do.

## What the client sees, and how current

The status comes from DOL's own case-status search. Per the [case lookup](/perm-case-status), every case is read once a day, every undecided case at least twice a day (three times on weekdays), and cases with an alert on them every 5 minutes on weekdays and every half hour at night and at weekends. It's a sweep, so a case decided since the last read shows at DOL first, and DOL stays the authority for any single case. [DOL's own case status search](https://flag.dol.gov/case-status-search) answers the same question directly.

## What it doesn't show

The lookup reads DOL's record, so it can't show your firm's work, anything at USCIS, or what DOL hasn't published yet.

- **Your firm's work.** Recruitment in progress, a draft RFI response, what you're waiting on from the client: DOL's record holds none of that.
- **USCIS.** After certification the I-140 is with USCIS, and its receipt number isn't a DOL case number.
- **Anything DOL hasn't published.** The wage, worksite and law firm on a pending case appear only once DOL publishes the case in its quarterly files.

The lookup can answer the "has anything happened?" emails. The questions that need judgment still come to you.
